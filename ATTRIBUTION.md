## Sources for the location research

Facts were checked against episode dialogue and summaries; the descriptions are written for this project
and do not copy text from these sources.

- Episode lists and summaries: Wikipedia, *Castlevania (TV series)* and *Castlevania: Nocturne*
- Castlevania Wiki (Fandom): pages for Lindenfeld, Brăila, the Belmont Hold, Machecoul, Olrox and episode pages
- Episode transcripts (transcripts.foreverdreaming.org) for distances, directions and short quotations
- Netflix Tudum articles on *Nocturne* Season 2 (setting and showrunner interviews)
- TVmaze episode guide; The Fandomentals (Season 2 finale); Siliconera (Nocturne opening)
- Lindenfeld village: prinbanat.ro and Wikipedia (*Buchin*)
